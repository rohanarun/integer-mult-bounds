## What to try next, and what not to repeat

1. **Leech:** jointly optimize the correction graph and a small binary-label realization. Scalar support below100 is impossible for this276-line slice, so more coordinates alone cannot improve that objective. A different100-support transfer might improve label topology. Then test proper-frame center gathering before building a full word.
2. **E7:** a specialized packet-sharing construction or changed decoder is needed. More global hosting/stage tuning of the retained generic R1505 factory cannot close its exact cost gap.
3. **Ternary:** test a changed decoder or an overcomplete factorization with legal nondegenerate center frames. The retained rank36 column-space construction is obstructed. Any survivor still needs dirty-helper cleanup, phases, a new global cover, recursive cost model, owners and an exact invoice.
4. **Current binary circuit:** structural word changes remain relevant. Pure owner packing of the fixed PR548 word has less than0.000400% possible relative kappa improvement by the separately archived exact dual extension. That ceiling does not apply to changed words.
