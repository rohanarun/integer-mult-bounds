## What changed in our understanding

The isotropic normal of the original 106-port gather is **not an obstruction** in the actual compiler. Its preimage-frame construction explicitly supports degenerate subspaces. Exact phase tests confirmed this. The binding issue is the copied-center cost, and the new cost certificate survives label changes and every recombination inside the original scalar rowspace.

The new full-shell transfer is f(t)=t(t^2-4)/48 for lattice inner product t. It preserves the diagonal and cancels the even off-diagonal interactions that made the natural Gram decoder incompatible. A divided-polynomial integer basis and 24 norm identities give an explicit 2,600-center dyadic factorization. Appending one zero bit makes every gather proper, and the generic Clifford interface accepts the resulting even ambient dimension 26.

Its optimistic center-copy screen is positive: N=98,280, C=65,000, and delta(L)=33,280L-98,280. That optimistic result **does not establish a competitive recurrence**. The conventional pure-source factory needs at least 98,280 source carriers plus 2,600 centers, and its unavoidable transition fees give the exact scoped ceiling b < 0.00137, below the pinned existing complex supplier near 0.001595. An independent certificate checks the normalization against a real PR558 owner/profile receipt and distinguishes local transition ranks from larger idle-child ranks.

## What remains worth testing

A useful next design must change the implementation, not merely search more seeds of the rejected constructions. Possibilities outside the certificates include coded or shared gather carriers, exploiting the Golay structure in a factored evaluation circuit, a different decoder, or a different hosting/controller construction. Each needs arbitrary dirty-register restoration and an actual paid profile before an outer kappa can be claimed.

No full billions-of-edges word was emitted merely to reconfirm a cost exclusion. The phase audits used exact polynomial-size Clifford calculations, not exponential state vectors. All batch jobs terminated. The small tuple/list harness failure in the cubic phase test and its correction are retained separately from mathematical rejections.
