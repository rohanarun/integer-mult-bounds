# Small Leech dirty-word route: rejected before construction

Date: 2026-10-12. This batch checks the missing prerequisite for adapting the existing E7 chronological dirty-register factory to the saved smaller Leech witnesses. It is an **exact structural rejection of that factory route**, not an admitted word or a bound on all Leech constructions.

## Exact obstruction

For the existing uniform odd-stage cover, with V ports, L stages and copied rank C, the per-replica deficit is

`delta(L) = (L−1)V − LC = L(V−C) − V`.

For the33-port witness, C>=62, so

`delta(L) <= −29L −33 <0` for every positive L.

The other two witnesses likewise have C>V. Thus no positive-saving moment admission is possible for these center factorizations in this cover even if all helper, idle, padding, phase and fallback costs are granted free. Increasing the stage count or improving hosting cannot repair a negative deficit.

The33-port factory also has a local rank-mass lower bound1894 before counting any delivery helpers. Dedicated delivery of its66 nonzero center-input coefficients would give73 initial helpers and rank mass3610 before any additional correction clones. Those costs are unnecessary to establish rejection; the copied-center deficit alone is decisive.

To make the33-port factorization worth reconsidering within this accounting, copied rank would have to fall from62 to at most32 for any stage count to admit a positive deficit, or at most29 at nine stages. This requires changing the factorization or how centers are delivered, rather than filling the missing implementation around the current factorization.

## Scope and stopping decision

Following the requested pre-build test, this route stops here. No chronological word, complete scalar endpoint, arbitrary-dirt restoration, full frame profile, phase compiler, global hosting or kappa was fabricated or claimed.

This does **not** reject Leech subsets in general. Different decoder factorizations, shared packet transformations, eliminating paid center copies, or a genuinely different supplier interface remain possible. It specifically rejects applying the existing E7 copied-center factory/cover to the three saved Leech factorizations.
